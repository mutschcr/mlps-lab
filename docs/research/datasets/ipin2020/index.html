Overview

The IPIN2020 Track 7 dataset is a benchmark for position estimation from channel impulse response measurements.

A roughly 15 × 20 m industrial environment with shelving and metal obstacles.

Year2020
ModalityUWB CIR
TaskLocalization
FormatHDF5

Measurement setup

Around 300,000 CIR observations over 1.5 h; approximately 230,000 train and 70,000 test observations.

Data format & contents

  • HDF5 ds with rec_time, burst_id, cir_real, cir_imag, anch_id, ref_x and ref_y.
  • UWB anchor nodes receive bursts from a mobile transmitter.
